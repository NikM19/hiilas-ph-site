Sessions & packages

My main goal is to make you a little happier and to create warm memories for you. I want the pricing to feel clear and calm, so here you can find an outline of my services. The final price may vary depending on the format, timing and location — we always talk everything through in advance, and I choose the best option for you personally.

If you need a portrait session, it’s a beautiful way to capture an important moment of your life. Just you, me and my camera. Shooting time starts from 1.5 hours, with at least 50 edited photos delivered. Price: 100 € (incl. VAT 25.5%).

If you want to capture the feelings between two people — a walk, a hug, a quiet moment together — a couple session / love story is perfect. Shooting time from 1.5 hours, with at least 100 photos delivered. Price: 150 € (incl. VAT 25.5%).

When you want everyone to be in the frame — a family session. Family is the most precious thing we have. Up to four people, shooting time from 1.5 hours, around 200 photos delivered. Price: 170 € (incl. VAT 25.5%).

If you are celebrating something special — a birthday, event or any meaningful day — a reportage session will capture the full atmosphere. I focus on emotions, details and natural moments. Shooting time from 1.5 hours, with at least 200 photos delivered. Price: 150 € (incl. VAT 25.5%).

If you have a special idea in mind: a creative project, business content, or something that’s hard to describe at first — let’s create it together. Price starts from 100 € (incl. VAT 25.5%).

For small, intimate celebrations — an elopement, when it’s just the two of you and your closest people, up to 15 guests. Shooting up to 5 hours, with at least 400 photos delivered. Price starts from 450 € (incl. VAT 25.5%).

By booking my services, you agree to my artistic editing style and light retouching. Please take some time to look through my portfolio to make sure my style resonates with you. I do not provide heavy retouching or RAW files. Final photos are delivered within 14 days for regular sessions and up to 30 days for elopements.

If you would like to book a session with me, feel free to write — we will talk everything through. Thank you for your time and your trust.
