About

Hi, I'm Anastasia Hiilas

Documentary photographer based in Helsinki.

Thank you for stopping here ♥

Documentary photography is the art of seeing the truth. At the heart of my approach are real emotions, honest glances, and genuine stories. During a photoshoot, I try to be your friend — someone who supports, encourages, and helps you feel comfortable and confident.

Every story is unique. If you think you’re not photogenic, let me show you how beautiful you truly are.

I believe that love is not only about two people. It’s about yourself, your family, friends, pets, your passion — about the moments worth remembering.

I love people through my camera. It doesn’t matter if you have experience in front of the lens — write to me, and together we’ll create photographs where you’ll recognize yourself and feel alive.

My story

Photography once saved me — it became a lighthouse on the darkest day.

Like many creative people, my inspiration was built on pain. But today, my home — photography — stands on a solid foundation. I know why and for whom I create.

I want people to see themselves through my eyes — real, alive, and beautiful, right where they are.

I’m a documentary photographer based in Helsinki, and my work isn’t about perfection or posing — it’s about life, the kind you can print, place in an album, and return to years later, remembering yourself happy and genuine in that very moment.

My purpose is to help you preserve memories and feel truly special.
